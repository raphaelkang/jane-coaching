About Jane

I've always been fascinated by people.

Why do we repeat the same patterns, even when we know they're not serving us? Why do capable, intelligent people sometimes become their own biggest obstacle?

Those questions have shaped both my career and the way I coach. Over the past two decades, I've worked across education, entrepreneurship, financial services, corporate strategy and operations, and executive coaching — teaching, training, coaching and supporting people through career transitions, leadership challenges and personal growth.

My approach combines internationally accredited coaching, NLP and psychology-informed practices with a person-centred, trauma-informed and neurodivergent-friendly lens. Rather than quick fixes or one-size-fits-all advice, I create a space where people can better understand themselves, challenge unhelpful patterns, and make intentional decisions that align with who they are.
